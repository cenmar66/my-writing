# Format

Built from archive/ only (21 pieces). Counts from `python3 tools/style_stats.py`.

## Length
- Median 218 words. Shortest 83 ("Your Attention Is the New Wealth"), longest 454 ("The City That Taught Me to Pay Attention").
- Most pieces land between 120 and 300 words. Two story-heavy pieces run past 400.

## Title and subtitle
- Title: 3 to 8 words, title case, a claim or an image. "Motivation Is a Houseguest." "The Chair at 7 PM." "Surrendering Is Not Quitting."
- Every piece has a subtitle. It carries the concrete hook: a year, a number, an object, a line of story. "2001. Defrauded by my partners. A 401K draining. Then my wife lifted my face and said one word."

## Sentences and paragraphs
- Median sentence 5 words. 90% are 10 words or fewer. Only 3 sentences in the archive pass 25 words.
- Median 2 sentences per paragraph. Lots of one-line paragraphs.
- Stacked lines with line breaks inside one paragraph for lists of things ("No office. No investors. No permission.").

## Headings and lists
- 18 of 21 pieces have no headings. The 3 longest use them: `####` short labels ("Arriving Empty", "The Dojo"), bold labels ("**The Moment It Hit**"), or numbered `###` reasons.
- Lists: 1 piece in 21 uses a numbered list. Lists show up as stacked fragment lines instead of bullets.

## Bold and italics
- Bold mostly for the closing question (11 of 21) and the "**Takeaway:**" line (3 of 21).
- Italics for book titles (*Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind*, *The Mastery Path*) and for one stressed word (*later*, *now*).

## How I end
- 19 of 21 end on a question to the reader. 11 of those are bold.
- 8 of 21 lead into it with "So let me ask you." Most recent pieces end on a bold question alone.
- 3 of 21 add a "**Takeaway:**" line before the question, lowercase after the colon.
- No sign-off, no CTA to subscribe, no "thanks for reading" written by me.

## Standard blocks
There is no fixed opening block. The closing block is the bold reader question, often after "So let me ask you."
The draft skill copies the closing from the latest piece in archive/ word for word in shape (not the question itself).
