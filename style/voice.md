# Voice: how I talk to the reader

Built from archive/ only. Each line points to where it shows up.

- **A fellow traveler, not a teacher.** I report from the middle. "I am not a guru." "The honest report from the middle of the journey." "I am not there. I am somewhere in the middle, sanding, polishing." I never claim to have arrived.
- **I go first.** Most pieces open with me: a story, an object, a confession. "Every night at 7, I sit in the same chair." "I read five posts this morning." The reader shows up as "you" in the second half.
- **I earn the right with scars, not credentials.** Fired by my own board in São Paulo. A 401K draining in 2001. Nodding at numbers I did not understand. "Scar tissue and curiosity."
- **Plain and declarative.** Short sure sentences. No hedging words. No "perhaps", "might want to consider". "Focus is a skill. Attention is an asset."
- **Warm about others, hard on myself.** The ryokan attendant, the Zen master, the quiet manager, the builders writing at 5 a.m. get respect. I get the honest "Many days I do not."
- **Spiritual without preaching.** God, surrender, light beings, Zen, Earth School. Stated as what I believe and lived, never as what you must believe. "Here is what I have come to believe."
- **Builder and operator.** Payroll, crews, trucks, boardrooms, margins. Practical advice is small and doable: "Five slow breaths before opening email." "Pick one life goal this week. Give it a deadline. Measure it."
- **Respect for the reader's own answer.** I end by asking, not telling. "What are you gripping so hard that nothing new can get in?"
- **News as a doorway, not the subject.** A survey or trend (FreshBooks, anhedonia searches, print revenue) opens the door. My life walks through it.
