# Retention checks for the judge

How my pieces open, move and end, written as yes/no checks. Built from archive/ only.
None of these are ranked by numbers yet (all pieces show 0 likes). They describe what I do, not yet what works best.
Rebuild them from the top pieces once style/winners.md is ranked.

## Opening (first 3 lines)
- [ ] Line 1 is a claim, a scene or an object in under 12 words. ("Motivation is a houseguest." "Every night at 7, I sit in the same chair." "I still have the first business book I ever bought.")
- [ ] By line 3 the reader knows the tension: the old way vs what I learned. ("I used to measure a day by how much I finished. / Now I measure it by how much mattered.")
- [ ] No throat clearing. No "In today's world", no definitions before the story, no "I want to talk about".
- [ ] A news hook, if any, sits in line 2 to 4, with its source in sources.md, and turns to me within two lines.

## Moving part to part
- [ ] Each paragraph follows from the one before. If you can swap two paragraphs and nothing breaks, one is filler.
- [ ] One story, told with concrete objects and a place (Tokyo, Hakone, São Paulo, the chair, the attic).
- [ ] A turn line between story and lesson: "Here is what changed." "The chair taught me something else." "People are learning what I learned the hard way."
- [ ] The lesson is said once, short, often as a "Not X. Y." pivot or a two-line contrast. Not repeated three ways.
- [ ] An admission of where I still fall short before the ending.

## Ending
- [ ] Ends on one question to the reader, about their life, answerable in a sentence.
- [ ] The question is bold, or follows "So let me ask you."
- [ ] Optional "**Takeaway:**" line before the question, one sentence.
- [ ] The last lines land on a short verdict, not a summary of the piece.
