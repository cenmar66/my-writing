## Habits

- **Spelled out, not contracted.** 111 spelled-out forms ("I am", "that is", "do not", "it is", "did not") against 8 contractions in the whole archive. "I do not know", never "I don't know".
- **"Not X. Y." pivots.** 16 in 21 pieces. "Not failure. Foundation." "Not the number. The searching." "Not in my head. Deep in my heart." "Not because they perform. Because they stopped hiding."
- **Three-beat fragment stacks**, often one per line: "Chair. / Page. / Breath." "The tea. / The breath. / The step." "Sit. / Breathe. / Notice. / Begin again." "Sore hands. Aching back. Tired legs."
- **Concrete objects instead of ideas.** A cracked 1989 Drucker paperback. A moisture meter in an attic. Tea going cold. A 401K draining. A futon, a tea tray, a single flower.
- **My own résumé as proof, in plain numbers.** $100 million export division. 40 years of business. Twenty-four years. Ingram Micro, Tokyo, SoftBank, Sparkle Restoration.
- **Admits the gap.** "Some days I live it. Many days I do not." "I am not there. I am somewhere in the middle." "I still lose it."
- **First person heavy.** Per 1000 words: I 43, my 12, me 7, you 18, your 6, we 1. I tell my story, then turn to "you" at the end.
- **Short.** Median sentence 5 words. 43% of sentences are 1 to 4 words.
