# AI Slop Made the Real Thing Rare

*A machine can sound like it knows. Only you can say you were there.*

I read five posts this morning.

Smooth sentences. Perfect structure. Nothing inside.

I could not tell who wrote them. I am not sure anyone did.

AI flooded the internet with perfect-sounding words.

And people noticed.

Consumers now say they trust human expertise more because of it. Most of them think that brands publish too much AI content.

The slop made the real thing rare.

Rare things become valuable. That is the trade.

I spent years on overnight flights. Tokyo. Singapore. Sao Paulo. Buenos Aires.

Land at dawn. Shower. Suit. Boardroom by nine.

Jet lag. Dry mouth. No sleep.

None of it could show.

Now I write the other way. No armor. No permission.

I am not against AI. I use it to brainstorm. I use it to edit.

I am against lazy thinking and unobserved living.

I share what I lived. The mistakes. The questions. The things I still do not understand.

That used to be just honesty.

Now the data says it is a strategy. I did not choose it for that. I chose it because I was tired of pretending.

A machine can sound like it knows.

Only you can say you were there.

Polish can be copied. Presence cannot.

So let me ask you.

**What is one thing you know from living it, not from reading about it?**
