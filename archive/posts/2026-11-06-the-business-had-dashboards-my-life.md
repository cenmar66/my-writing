# A Strategy for Your Life

*Executives build five-year plans for companies. Almost none build one for their own lives. I was one of them.*

Executives spend hundreds of hours building strategy for their companies.

Goals. Margins. Five-year plans.

Almost none of them build one for their own lives.

A Wharton professor teaches exactly that. A personal strategy. Run with the same rigor as the business plan.

I spent years running a business like a machine and living like an accident.

The business had dashboards. My life had momentum.

Strategy belongs in both.

Pick one life goal this week. Give it a deadline. Measure it.

The business is not the only thing worth running well.

What deserves a strategy in your life?
