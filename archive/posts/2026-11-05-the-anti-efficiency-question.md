# The Anti-Efficiency Question

*I used to measure a day by how much I finished. Now I ask one quieter question, and half my calendar falls away.*

I used to measure a day by how much I finished.

Now I measure it by how much mattered.

Somewhere along the way I started asking a different question.

Not how do I fit more in.

Instead: is this the best use of where I am right now?

Right now. This energy. This hour. This version of me.

Most of what fills a day does not move anything forward. The instant replies. The meetings with no point. The busywork dressed as progress.

Anti-efficiency is not laziness. It is honesty.

Fewer things. Done with care. Beats more things done in a rush.

Every single time.

Try the question for one day. Watch what falls away.

**What would you stop doing first?**
