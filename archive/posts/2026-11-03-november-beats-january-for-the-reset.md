# November Beats January for the Reset

*My evenings got sloppy this summer. September and October are gone. November is not a failure. It is just a later bell.*

I stopped trusting January a long time ago.

The gym crowds. The vision boards. The big promises.

By February, the whole show is over.

A psychologist named Karen Stewart says September works better. But if you missed September, I say November works almost as well.

Our brains still run on the old school calendar.

Back to school. Back to rhythm. Back to real life.

Her prescription is a soft reset.

Not a new me. The old me, with my routines back.

I like this.

The Mastery Path was never about heroic makeovers.

It is about small practices, repeated daily.

My evenings got sloppy this summer. September and October are gone. Can I still regain my sanity before the end of the year?

I think so. November is not a failure. It is just a later bell.

Here is what sloppy looked like for me. Late dinners in front of a screen. The notebook on the desk, closed for weeks. One more episode. Then bed at midnight, wired and empty.

No permission needed to start again. No Monday. No first of the month. A soft reset can begin on any ordinary evening. One routine. One hour. One small door back to myself.

Tonight I take back my writing hour.

**What is one routine you want back this week?**
