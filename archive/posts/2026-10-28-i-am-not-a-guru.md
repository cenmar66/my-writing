# I Am Not a Guru

*Forty years of business. A marriage that survived. Still no answers. Just an honest report from the middle.*

I am not a guru.

Let me say it plainly. The internet has enough of them.

I have not figured out life. I am still figuring it out. At an age when I am supposed to have answers.

What I have is scar tissue and curiosity.

40 years of business experience.  
A marriage that survived.  
Three kids who turned out well.  
A meditation cushion with my name on it.

That is not authority. That is experience.

There is a difference.

Early in my career I sat in a boardroom and nodded at numbers I did not understand. Nobody caught it. I walked out feeling like I had won.

I had not. I had just added another plate of armor.

Gurus sell certainty. I am selling the opposite. The honest report from the middle of the journey.

Every time you pretend to have it figured out, the armor thickens. Every time you admit you do not know, it thins.

People can smell performance. They can also smell honesty. One repels. The other draws people in.

Not performance. Presence.

So I will keep writing from the middle of the mess.

Not above it.
Not past it.
In it.

Fellow traveler. That is the only title I want.

So let me ask you. What are you pretending to know right now?
