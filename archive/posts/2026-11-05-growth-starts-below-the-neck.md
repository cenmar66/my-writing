# Growth Starts Below the Neck

*Twenty years of adrenaline taught me this. You cannot think your way out of a body that believes it is being chased.*

Your nervous system sets the ceiling.

Searches for vagus nerve reset are up 123% in three months.

I spent twenty years running on adrenaline. Corporate meetings. Red-eye flights. Strategic planning report. Payroll. I called it drive.

My body called it something else. Tight jaw. Shallow breath. Awake at 3 a.m. Putting the finishes touches to a 9 am presentation.

I read every book on mindset. I journaled. I set intentions. None of it held, because I was trying to think my way out of a body that believed it was being chased.

People are learning what I learned the hard way.

Not from a book. From a hospital bed, or close to it. My doctor looked at my numbers and said, "You're not stressed. You're stuck."

So I started small. Five slow breaths before opening email. Cold water on my face in the morning. Ten minutes on the porch with no phone. It felt too simple to matter.

It mattered. The jaw loosened. The late night alarms in my head got quieter. I still had payroll. I still had the board. But I met them from a different place.

No amount of mindset work fixes a body stuck in survival mode.

You start below the neck. Slow breath. Cold water. Stillness.

Calm the body first. The mind follows.

**What does your body need today?**
