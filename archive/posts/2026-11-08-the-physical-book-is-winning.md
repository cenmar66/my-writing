# The Physical Book Is Winning

*A cracked 1989 Drucker paperback, a 75% jump in print sales, and why I am writing my next books for paper first.*

I still have the first business book I ever bought. The Effective Executive by Peter Drucker. A used paperback. 1989. Cracked spine. A coffee stain on the cover.

My notes fill the margins. Blue ink and arrows. Underlines on every other page. When I open it, I meet the man I was at 23. No screen has ever given me that.

I am not alone. Print revenue grew 75% this year among indie publishers. The strongest growth of any format.

I'm not talking about ebooks or audio. I'm talking about print.

Readers are choosing books as objects. The feel and smell of paper. The weight in their hands. The margins to write in.

Printed books hold about 71% of all book revenue in 2026. The industry calls it the analog reset. I call it a desire for things that last.

A screen refreshes. A book remains where you place it.

I am writing my books for paper. *The Mastery Path*. *Mold Confidential*. *Home Safe Home*. Paper editions first.

Words I hope will sit on shelves for decades, and not disappear in a feed.

**What books are you glad exist on paper?**
