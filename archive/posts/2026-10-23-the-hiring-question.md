# The Hiring Question

*86% of solopreneurs now try AI before hiring a human. The real question is what deserves a human.*

Growth used to mean hiring.

Your first hire was the rite of passage. Proof you made it.

A new FreshBooks survey says 86% of solopreneurs now try AI before they hire a human.

Ninety-eight percent of those using AI say it helped them hit goals they could not reach alone.

Here is what changed.

Hiring is no longer the only growth lever. It is a choice between tools and people, made on purpose.

I ran a business with crews, trucks, and payroll for decades. My next chapter runs lean.

Tools do the heavy lifting. People do what only people do.

The question is no longer when do I hire.

The question is what deserves a human.

**The question for you: what in your work deserves a human?**
