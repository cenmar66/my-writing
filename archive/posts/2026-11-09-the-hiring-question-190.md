# The Hiring Question

*86% of solopreneurs now try AI before they hire a person. So what still deserves a human?*

Business growth used to mean hiring.

Your first hire was a rite of passage. Proof you made it.

A new FreshBooks survey says 86% of solopreneurs now try AI before they hire a person.

Ninety-eight percent of those using AI say it helped them hit goals they could not reach alone.

So something has shifted.

Hiring used to be the only way to grow. Now it is a choice. Software or a person.

I remember my first hire at Sparkle Restoration. A young surfer guy with lots of energy and calloused hands. I signed his paycheck before I signed my own.

That paycheck meant something. To him. To me.

Now a business owner's first hire might be software. No interview. No handshake. No payroll.

I've run a business with crews, trucks, and payroll for decades. My next chapter runs lean.

More and more, software carries the load. People do what only people can do.

So the question changes. Not when do I hire, but what deserves a human.
