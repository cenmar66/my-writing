# The Blue-Collar Renaissance

*A truck, some tools, and stubbornness. Twenty-four years later, the culture is catching up to the trades.*

I started my last company with a truck, some tools, and stubbornness.

No office.  No investors.  No permission.

I remember those long days like they were yesterday.

Sore hands.  Aching back.  Tired legs.

And the quiet smile that only a job well done can bring.

I had earned enough to shelter and feed my wife and children.

Something grew in me in those years.  A confidence that no matter what, I could take care of them.

Twenty-four years later, the culture is catching up.

Parents used to apologize when their kid chose a trade.

Now more of them are seeing the wisdom of it.

Young people want work that will still matter after AI arrives.

AI will not crawl into your attic with a moisture meter.

It will not pull up flooded carpet at midnight.

It will not look a scared homeowner in the eye and say, "We've got this."

Real work has a moat no algorithm crosses.

I am writing a book on building businesses like this.

The lesson holds in any era.

Skill you own beats a title you rent.

So let me ask you.

**What is one skill you own that no software can take?**
