# Why Self-Mastery Matters Now

*I could run a $100 million division. I could not run my own attention. A short note for anyone who leads anything.*

I ran a $100 million export division. I could manage sales, logistics, payroll, and crises.

I could not manage my own attention.

I checked email before my feet touched the floor. I answered calls at dinner. I had answers for every person in the building.

None for myself.

This is for you if your phone decides your mornings. If the news decides your mood. If you lead a team, a family, a project, or just your own day, and you feel it slipping.

Self-mastery used to be a luxury.

Now it is the floor.

### 1. The noise got louder

Every app is built by smart people paid to take your focus. They are very good at their jobs.

If you do not train your mind, someone else will.

### 2. The ground keeps moving

Jobs change. Tools change. Titles disappear overnight. I have seen whole divisions erased in one board meeting.

Nobody can restructure what you carry inside.

### 3. People watch how you hold yourself

In a tense meeting, the calmest person sets the temperature.

I watched one quiet manager steady a room of forty. No speech. No slides. Just breath and patience.

Not performance. Presence.

### The work itself is simple

Sit.
Breathe.
Notice.
Begin again.

Simple is not the same as easy. Your mind will wander a hundred times. You bring it back a hundred times. That is the practice.

**Zen mind = Beginner's mind.**

Pick up *Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind* by Shunryu Suzuki. He is one of my favorite Zen teachers. The book is short. I keep a copy by the bed. Read a page or two at a time, slowly.

You do not need permission to start. You do not need a retreat or a guru. You need ten minutes and the honesty to stay.

The world will not get quieter.

You can.

So let me ask you. Who is running your mind today?
