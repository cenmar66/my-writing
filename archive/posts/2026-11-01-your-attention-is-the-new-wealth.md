# Your Attention Is the New Wealth

*I used to spend my focus on noise. Then I started guarding it like money, and the returns surprised me.*

Your attention is the new wealth.

Attention spans keep shrinking. Everything around you is built to break your focus into pieces.

I used to trade my focus for noise. Notifications. News feeds. Another tab.

Then I started guarding it like money.

An hour of deep work before I pull out my phone. One task at a time. Silence to start the day.

The returns beat any portfolio I ever managed.

Focus is a skill. Attention is an asset.

How are you investing yours?
