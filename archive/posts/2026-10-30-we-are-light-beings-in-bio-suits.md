# We Are Light Beings in Bio-Suits

*A short note on armor, sanding, and the people who walk into a room and change the temperature.*

Underneath the armor, you are light. I am still learning to believe it.

Here is what I have come to believe. Each of us carries a bright inner light. Around it, we build a thick outer core. The ego. The worries. The endless mental chatter.

The core is armor. It protected us once. But it also covers the light.

The work of a lifetime, as I see it, is sanding down that armor. Every honest conversation thins the armor a little. Every act of kindness. Every morning you sit still instead of scrolling.

As the outer core thins, you become translucent. People can see your light. They feel drawn to you and they cannot explain why.

I have met translucent people. My zen teacher in Tokyo was as bright as the sun. His warmth embraced my soul and gave me peace.

You have met these kind of people too. They walk into a room and the temperature changes. Not because they perform. Because they stopped hiding.

I am not there. I am somewhere in the middle, sanding, polishing.

Some days the armor feels thick. Old fears. Old stories. The whiny mind, doing its laps.

But I keep sanding and polishing. That is the work.

**Takeaway: **you do not need to become someone else. You need to remove what covers who you already are.
