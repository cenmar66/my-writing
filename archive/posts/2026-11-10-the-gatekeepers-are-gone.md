# The Gatekeepers Are Gone

*I signed other people's checks for years. Now I am signing my own name to the page. Nobody gave me permission.*

The gatekeepers are gone. I am writing my own books now.

For years I signed other people's checks. Board meetings. Strategic planning retreats. A $100 million export division. A multinational tech startup. I never once signed my own name to a page of prose I had written.

There was another working life, too. Thirty years in flooded crawl spaces and burnt attics.

Now I sit anywhere I can find a little free time. Before work. After dinner. Between appointments. Coffee and a laptop. A stack of notes and memories from both lives.

Nobody gave me permission. I just started. The door was never locked. I just never checked.

Self-published books now outnumber traditionally published ones by more than 5 to 1.

Some days the page stays blank for an hour. I read the same note three times. I delete more than I keep.

That is the trade. No editor to blame. No advance to spend. Just you, the work, and the reader you have not met yet.

I am no expert at this. I am a student.

**What book is sitting inside you?**
