# Surrendering Is Not Quitting

*2001. Defrauded by my partners. A 401K draining. Then my wife lifted my face and said one word.*

This story is for builders who believe every outcome depends on them.

I believed that too.
Then life broke me open.

**The Moment It Hit**

2001. My partners in my international tech startup defrauded me.

No job. Dwindling savings. A 401K draining month by month to feed my family.

I did what builders do. I pushed harder.

Sleepless nights. Tired body. Empty hands.

One evening my wife lifted my face with both hands. She looked into my eyes and said:

"Surrender. Ask God to take over."

**What I Got Wrong**

I thought God only helped those who did everything themselves.

Effort in. Outcome out.
A simple equation. That simplicity fooled me.

I heard her that night. I did not listen for days.

Surrender sounded like losing.
Like a white flag.
Like a man who stopped trying.

Then I let go. Not in my head. Deep in my heart.

A path opened. A new business opportunity.
A quiet force within lightened my burden.

I knew it then.
I would not lack.
My family would be cared for.

**How It Plays Out Now**

That was 24 years ago.

My business has had its ups and downs since. Normal ones.

But I have never felt alone.
Never in despair about the future.

When I remember to trust, the phone rings.
New clients. Old clients. People looking for help.

When I forget, I grip. I push. I tire.
Then I remember again.

Effort still matters. Control does not.

That is the trade.

Surrender is not quitting.
It is making room.

**So let me ask you. What are you gripping so hard that nothing new can get in?**
