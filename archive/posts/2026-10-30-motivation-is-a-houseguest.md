# Motivation Is a Houseguest

*I waited years to feel like writing. Then I stopped waiting and built a container: 7 to 8, every night, same chair.*

Motivation is a houseguest.  
  
It shows up. Eats your food. Leaves.  
  
I waited on it for years. Waited to feel like writing.   
  
The feeling rarely came.  
  
So I built a container instead. 7 to 8. Every night.  
  
Chair. Laptop. Door closed.  
  
Some nights the words flow. Some nights they crawl. Same chair. Same hour.  
  
I run a business. Payroll does not wait for me to feel ready. Tennis, meditation, the gym, the company. None of it runs on inspiration. All of it runs on a calendar.  
  
People ask how I stay disciplined. I am not disciplined. I am designed.   
  
Willpower is a battery. Design is a power grid.  
  
Here is the blueprint:  
  
One thing that matters.  
A time.  
A place.  
  
Protect it like a meeting with someone you respect.  
  
Then show up. Especially when you do not want to.  
  
So let me ask you. What is your one thing? And where does it live?
