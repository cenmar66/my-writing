# Discipline Loves Company

*Big office. Closed door. I called the silence focus. Mostly it was isolation. What changed when I let people back in.*

Body doubling is trending. That is working side by side with another person to stay focused.

It sounds simple. It works.

I write more when someone else is writing nearby. I think clearer when I say it out loud to a friend.

Discipline loves company. Growth loves witnesses.

For years I worked alone at the top. Big office. Closed door. Quiet hallway. I told myself the silence was focus. Mostly it was isolation.

The best work I ever did happened in rooms full of people. A bustling open space office at midnight. A kitchen table with my wife. A dojo where nobody spoke, but everybody showed up.

You do not need to do the hard thing alone. Who walks with you?
